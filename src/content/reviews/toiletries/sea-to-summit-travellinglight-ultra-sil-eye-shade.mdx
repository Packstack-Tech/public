## Overview

The TravellingLight Ultra-Sil Eye Shade is a stripped-down sleep mask: quilted Ultra-Sil Cordura nylon on the outside, soft microfiber against the skin, a nose baffle, and an elastic strap with no Velcro. At 23 g it adds almost nothing to a pack, and it ships with a set of earplugs. The trade-off is a compact cut that blocks light well for many sleepers and leaks it around the edges for others.

## Performance

Light blocking is the job here, and most owners say the mask does it. Buyers describe it as surprisingly effective for something this small, and one Bergfreunde reviewer was struck by how much light it shuts out. Its natural habitat is the summer high latitudes. A traveler who first bought one in Alaska to sleep through the night-time sun is typical, and a Finnish buyer called it perfect for Arctic midnight-sun nights. Campers use it the same way in a bright single-wall tent when first light arrives around 4:30 a.m.

Comfort is the more divisive question. The mask is thin and low-profile, so side sleepers report it doesn't get in the way, and owners who wear it nightly say it stays unobtrusive and doesn't ache the ears by morning. The strap is plain elastic with no hook-and-loop closure, so there is nothing to snag hair or stick to a pillow. Some owners like the nose baffle for sealing the gap that sneaks light in on cheaper masks.

The complaints cluster around fit. A number of REI reviewers say the mask is too small, even on narrow faces, and that light creeps in at the edges because the surface area and contouring are modest. Others say it presses on the eyes, particularly when the strap is cinched tight, and a few find the strap adjuster fiddly. A separate group of owners, including one who has arthritis in her hands, say the adjustment is easy once learned. The mask's overall REI average is 3.8 out of 5 across 38 reviews, a lukewarm score that reflects this split rather than any failure of materials. The same pattern shows up elsewhere: European retailers show higher scores and strong recommend rates, while a handful of buyers called it uncomfortable or too small.

Durability is mostly a non-issue, with owners noting sturdy stitching and years of regular use. One critical REI reviewer reported that the rubbery printed ink on the strap turned sticky after roughly a dozen uses, which looks like a one-off rather than the norm. Another buyer reported it dropping out of a travel-pillow pocket on a first trip, which is the kind of loss a 23 g item invites. The included earplugs are a small bonus that buyers mention with approval.

On the trail, the mask does double duty as a plane and hostel sleep aid. It packs flat enough to vanish into a quilt stuff sack or a toiletries pouch, and at 23 g it costs less weight than a spare pair of socks. Bergfreunde lists it at about €17, a fair price for a washable, reusable mask.

## Pros & Cons

**Pros**

- 23 g (0.8 oz) and flat when packed
- Effective at blocking midnight sun and early-morning tent light for many users
- Nose baffle helps seal the gap around the bridge
- Low-profile enough for side sleepers
- No Velcro, so nothing to snag hair or pillow fabric
- Earplugs included
- Washable and reusable

**Cons**

- Compact cut lets light leak around the edges for some faces
- Can press on the eyes, especially with the strap tightened
- Strap adjustment frustrates some owners
- Isolated report of the strap print turning tacky
- Mixed marks overall, with a 3.8 out of 5 average on REI

## Who Should Buy This

Backpackers and thru-hikers who sleep in bright tents, hike in northern latitudes during summer, or share hostel dorms and buses will get real use from it, especially those who prefer a thin mask over a bulky contoured one. Side sleepers and anyone averse to Velcro straps are the best fit. Hikers with larger faces, those who need total blackout, or anyone sensitive to pressure on the eyelids should look at a deeper, molded-cup mask and accept the extra grams.

## Verdict

The TravellingLight Eye Shade is a cheap, featherweight fix for light-induced sleeplessness, and for the many sleepers whose faces suit its cut, it does the job without notice. The fit lottery keeps it from a higher score. Buy it for the weight and the simplicity, and keep the receipt if the edges leak.
