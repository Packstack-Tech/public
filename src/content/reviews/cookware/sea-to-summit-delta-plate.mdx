## Overview

The Sea to Summit Delta Plate is a 23 cm, 1-liter polypropylene plate with raised walls, a honeycomb base and a thumb grip that doubles as a hang loop. It was built for car campers, overlanders and short backpacking trips where a real meal on a real plate beats eating out of a pot. The trade-off is plain: at 130 g and a full 23 cm across, it's a comfort item in a category where most thru-hikers carry nothing at all.

## Performance

**Heat handling.** The hex pattern on the underside is the plate's signature feature. It cuts material, and so weight, while spreading surface heat so the plate can be held with hot food in it. Owners describe the base as staying comfortable to hold, and one European buyer singled out its ability to keep food warm outdoors. The thumb grip keeps fingers out of the food, and it earns repeat praise for making a loaded plate easy to carry across camp.

**Capacity and shape.** At 1,000 ml, the Delta Plate is closer to a shallow bowl than a dinner plate. The 4 cm walls hold a full dehydrated meal, a pile of pasta or a runny sauce without slopping, and owners call the lip "clutch" for sauces. The flat interior is also big enough for light food prep. Sea to Summit's own suggestion is to use it as a cheese board, and one owner reported cutting on it without trouble. The inner curves are shaped to fit the company's Delta cutlery, and the interior marks give rough volume measurements for rehydrating meals.

**Durability.** This is where the plate earns its reputation. The material is rigid and cut resistant, and buyers consistently call it sturdy and tough. An overlander who carried four of them for six months on the road, after several earlier years of on-and-off use, reported no durability problems. That same user ate off them at -20 °C in Norway without trouble. Washing up in that cold was the hard part, not the plate. The surface resists odors and stains, wipes clean quickly and survives the dishwasher at home.

**Packability.** This is the weak spot. A 23 cm rigid disc with a 4 cm lip takes real room in a pack. Owners who like the plate still concede it isn't compact, and one pair of buyers noted the plates don't nest as tightly as they'd like, so a stack takes up more room than the plate's shape suggests. Others found the stack acceptable for van and RV storage. In a backpack, the plate works best as a lid or divider for a pot or sits flat against the back panel.

**Carabiner loop.** The thumb grip hole lets the plate hang from a pack or a carabiner and can clip cutlery to it. It's a useful touch for basecamp or a vehicle, but it's not a feature that changes the weight math on the trail.

## Pros & Cons

**Pros**

- Tough, rigid and cut resistant; survives years of abuse
- Hex base keeps the underside comfortable to hold with hot food
- 1-liter capacity with raised walls handles big meals and sauces
- Thumb grip and hang loop are genuinely useful
- Easy to clean, resists stains and odors, dishwasher and microwave safe
- Large flat floor works for food prep or as a cutting surface

**Cons**

- 130 g is heavy for a plate in a lightweight kit
- 23 cm diameter is bulky inside a pack
- Stacks don't nest as tightly as the shape suggests
- Rigid plastic offers no insulation for keeping food warm over a long meal

## Who Should Buy This

Car campers, overlanders, van and RV travelers, and weekend backpackers who cook real meals and want a plate that will outlast the rest of the kitchen kit. It also suits couples and families who want two or four matching plates that survive the dishwasher. Thru-hikers and gram-counters should look elsewhere. Eating from a pot or a freezer bag saves the full 130 g, and a smaller bowl or mug covers a solo hiker's needs without eating pack volume.

## Verdict

The Delta Plate is a well-made, durable plate that's pleasant to eat from and hard to break, and its heat-dispersing base does its job. But it's built for comfort, not for minimalism, and at 130 g and 23 cm across it's a poor fit for an ultralight base weight. Buy it for camp and vehicle kitchens, and skip it for long-distance hiking.
