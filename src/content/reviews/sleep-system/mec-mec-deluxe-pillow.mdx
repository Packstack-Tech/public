## Overview

The MEC Deluxe Pillow is a hybrid inflatable — an air-filled body with a thin open-cell foam topper bonded to the top surface, all wrapped in a brushed polyester knit shell. It sits in the middle ground between the bare-minimum ultralight inflatable and a full compressible foam pillow: more comfortable than the former, more packable than the latter. It's aimed at backpackers and car campers who don't want to sacrifice sleep quality but still need something that fits in a corner of their pack.

## Performance

### Comfort & Loft

The 12 cm (about 5 inches) of loft is the headline number here, and it matters. <cite index="12-4">At 5 inches thick, it's taller than most standard backpacking pillows</cite>, which makes a real difference if you're a side sleeper who typically wakes up with their ear pressed to the sleeping pad mat by 3 a.m. <cite index="9-1,9-2">It's well suited for side sleepers who need a thicker, firmer pillow, though back and stomach sleepers can partially deflate it to dial in a lower profile.</cite>

The foam topper is the key differentiator from a plain air pillow. <cite index="2-8">It's made of open-cell foam scraps left over from sleeping pad construction</cite> — a clever use of offcuts. <cite index="9-5">The foam topper makes the surface genuinely soft and conforming</cite> rather than that familiar squishy-balloon feel you get from a bladder-only design. <cite index="12-5">It also adds a touch of warmth</cite>, and the brushed knit shell deserves its own mention: <cite index="19-3">the fabric warmed up quickly against the cheek, so the cold-pillow problem that plagues many inflatables was barely noticeable.</cite>

That said, keep your expectations calibrated. <cite index="19-4,19-5">Since most of the pillow is still air, it can't quite match the comfort of a premium all-foam option like the HEST or Therm-a-Rest Compressible Cinch — but it's noticeably more comfortable than a standard backpacking inflatable at a similar price, weight, and packed size.</cite> If you crave a fluffy, cloud-like feel, this isn't it. <cite index="9-7">It offers lots of volume but not much "fluff" — firm and supportive, not soft and billowy.</cite>

### Inflation & Valve

<cite index="8-2">
  The pillow blows up easily and the check valve prevents air loss while you're
  filling it.
</cite>
In practice it takes three to five full breaths to reach a firm inflation —
nothing to complain about. Firmness is continuously adjustable, so you can
soften it down for back sleeping or top it off for side sleeping.
<cite index="10-3">
  You can inflate it as much as you want to adjust its firmness and height.
</cite>
Deflation is a simple press of the valve.

### Packability

<cite index="9-6">It compacts down to roughly the size of a softball.</cite>
That's genuinely small for a pillow with this much loft.
<cite index="8-4">
  Getting it back into the stuff sack looks intimidating at first, but folding
  it lengthwise and rolling it up gets it back in without a fight.
</cite>
For a product that inflates to roughly the footprint of a standard bed pillow,
the packed volume is impressive.

### The Sliding Problem

One consistent complaint from real-world users is worth flagging plainly: <cite index="17-16">this pillow has a tendency to slide away during the night.</cite> The smooth knit shell and TPU interior don't grip a sleeping pad or sleeping bag shell particularly well. <cite index="17-17">One workaround is to slip your sleeping pad inside a spare T-shirt and tuck the pillow into the same T-shirt at the top — the fabric holds both together so the pillow can't wander.</cite> It's an inelegant fix, but it works. MEC doesn't include any attachment system (no Velcro tabs, no pad-locking straps), which feels like a missed design opportunity given that competitors like Sea to Summit include proprietary pillow-lock systems.

### Size Choice

<cite index="8-1">
  The weight difference between medium and large is only about 54 grams
</cite>
— roughly the weight of a couple of energy chews. Unless you're deep in
base-weight obsession territory, the large is the more practical choice for most
sleepers given the extra surface area, particularly if you move around at night.

## Pros & Cons

**Pros**

- Genuine hybrid comfort — the foam topper meaningfully improves on a plain air pillow
- 12 cm loft works well for side sleepers without over-engineering
- Packed size is impressively small (~softball) for the loft delivered
- Brushed polyester knit is soft against skin and resists the cold-cheek effect
- Simple one-valve design; nothing to break or lose
- Comes with a stuff sack

**Cons**

- Slides around on smooth sleeping pad surfaces; no attachment system included
- Comfort ceiling is lower than all-foam options (HEST, Therm-a-Rest Compressible Cinch)
- Firm feel won't satisfy sleepers who prefer a soft, fluffy pillow
- Open-cell foam topper isn't removable or washable
- Primarily available through MEC (Canadian co-op), which limits accessibility for US-based buyers

## Who Should Buy This

The MEC Deluxe Pillow is the right call for backpackers and weekend campers who sleep on their side, want something substantially more comfortable than a basic inflatable, but aren't ready to carry the weight (or spend the money) of a premium foam pillow. It's also a strong choice for travelers — anyone packing light for a hut trip, a long flight, or a hostel stay will appreciate how small it gets. If you're an ultralight purist counting every gram, there are lighter options; if you want true memory-foam comfort, spend more on the Therm-a-Rest Compressible Cinch. Everyone in between is the target audience here.

## Verdict

The MEC Deluxe Pillow delivers a meaningful comfort upgrade over a standard inflatable without blowing your weight budget or your pack space. The foam topper and soft shell are genuine improvements, the loft is appropriate for side sleeping, and it packs down to almost nothing. The sliding problem is real and the comfort still plateaus below a full foam pillow — but at this price and weight, it's a hard package to argue with. **7/10.**
