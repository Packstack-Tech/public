## Overview

The Expedition Hood 10 is a detachable lid for Whippa's Wollemi 90 roll-top pack. It adds about 10 liters of storage for 115 g, and it comes off when the trip doesn't need it. It's a narrow product with a clear job: more room for a week-plus carry, or a dry, quick-access pocket on top of the load. The trade-off is that it only suits one pack and costs about as much as a decent stuff sack collection.

## Performance

**Capacity and access.** The Wollemi 90 is a roll-top pack. Of its 90 L, about 82 L sits in the main body and the rest in the front stash pocket, so a bulky food carry or a packrafting kit can leave the roll-top maxed out. The hood fills that gap. Owners who fit it to the Wollemi describe a pack that reaches roughly 100 L. The rear zipper gives access to the hood contents without unrolling the main bag. That matters on a wet day, when the alternative is opening the whole pack to reach a map or a snack.

**Weather protection.** The hood follows the same construction as the pack. Its seams are taped, and the zipper is water-repellent rather than a true waterproof closure. Reviewers who have tested the Wollemi itself, in Tasmanian rain and scrub, describe the pack as essentially waterproof thanks to its taped seams and coated fabrics. The hood is built to that standard. Anyone carrying something that must never see moisture should still put it in a dry bag, because a zipper that repels water is not one that seals out a submerged pack.

**Attachment.** Fitting takes a few minutes. The side straps that normally hold the roll-top come off, the two roll-top ends clip back onto themselves, and the same straps then run to the front of the hood. Two toggles at the back of the hood connect to the small accessory loops on the pack. Whippa designed the hood to leave a gap between it and the pack body, so a climbing or packrafting helmet can sit underneath or a rope can be slung through. For canyoning, packrafting and alpine trips, that is the detail that separates the hood from a generic lid.

**Weight and durability.** At 115 g, the hood is a rounding error against a pack that weighs 1.16 kg in its base configuration. One Wollemi owner who weighed the whole kit measured the hood at 105 g, with each side pocket at 55 g. The Ultra 200X body is a tougher cloth than the featherweight fabrics common in this category. Wollemi owners praise the pack's resistance to abrasion in off-track scrub. The hood sits at the top of the pack and takes less of that abuse than the main body does. Reviewers have no long-term data on the hood alone, and it has no independent test reviews of its own.

**Price.** Whippa lists the hood at about A$149, and retailers show it from roughly A$107 depending on fabric and market. That is a lot for 10 L of nylon. Anyone who already owns the Wollemi and routinely overpacks, though, will find it cheaper than buying a bigger pack.

## Pros & Cons

**Pros**

- Adds about 10 L for 115 g, which is less than the weight of a full water bottle's worth of gear.
- Taped seams and a water-repellent zipper match the Wollemi's weatherproof construction.
- Zippered rear access to the top of the load without opening the roll-top.
- Leaves room to carry a helmet or a rope between the hood and pack.
- Detaches in minutes, so the pack drops back to its normal profile on shorter trips.
- Made in Australia, with a manufacturer that owners say responds quickly.

**Cons**

- Built around the Wollemi 90, so it has no use without that pack.
- The zipper is water-repellent, not waterproof.
- Pricey for its size, and prices vary between retailers.
- Owners have little track record for this accessory on its own.
- Adds a little height, which matters for head clearance when scrambling.

## Who Should Buy This

Wollemi 90 owners who plan expedition-length trips in remote country should buy it. That means packrafters, off-track walkers in Tasmania, and anyone who needs to carry 10 days of food plus bulky gear. It also suits people who want a top pocket for quick access in rain. The Wollemi's base pack already tops out at 82 L in the main body, so it is the sensible fix for a bag that fills before the load does. Anyone carrying a Wollemi on trips of three or four days, or hikers on a different pack, should skip it. A hood only helps if the pack can accept it.

## Verdict

The Expedition Hood 10 is a well-thought-out accessory that does what it claims for 115 g and a short attachment routine. It is a sound buy for existing Wollemi owners who regularly run out of room, and an unnecessary one for everyone else.
