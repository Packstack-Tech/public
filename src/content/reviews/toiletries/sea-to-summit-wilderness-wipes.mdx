## Overview

Sea to Summit's Wilderness Wipes are rinse-free body wipes made from a thick viscose non-woven fabric. They are meant for the end of a sweaty day, when a shower is several days off. They clean better than a baby wipe and hold up better under use. The trade-off is weight: the 36-count pack weighs 292 g, so most ultralight hikers will want to decant it.

## Performance

**Cleaning.** The common thread in owner reports is that these wipes cut through sweat and trail grime without leaving a film. A Grand Canyon rim-to-rim hiker on REI's site described a full-body cleanup from a single wipe, though the wipe was nearly dry by the end. A Utah camper who spent 4–5 days at a time without a shower, in 90°F-plus heat, reported no sticky or tacky feeling afterward. Paddling Light's tester settled on one wipe for face, underarms, groin and feet, and said two work much better. The 6 x 8 in size is enough for that routine, but a full-body wash-down takes more than one wipe.

**Durability.** This is the main reason to buy these over a drugstore pack. Customers on Backcountry and Public Lands say the wipes don't fall apart in the hand. Outside's writer, who used them on an Alaskan packraft trip, called them substantially thicker and stronger than ordinary wet wipes. That strength matters for the less glamorous jobs, and the same writer used them to clean up after a bowel movement. One owner who ran out of toilet paper at a Montana outhouse used them for the same purpose.

**Skin feel and scent.** The formula is alcohol-free and pH balanced. Testers describe the scent as light and clean rather than perfumed. Several owners with sensitive skin or allergies report no irritation. The reports aren't uniform: one six-day hiker disagreed with other reviewers that the wipes leave no residual feeling on the skin. Cold-weather users have some praise too. One expedition user reported the wipes were less prone to freezing than other brands.

**Packaging.** This is the most consistent complaint. Owners on REI and Smarter Travel say the resealable pouch doesn't always seal well, and a wipe left exposed dries out. One buyer tore the pouch open on first use. Anyone carrying these on a trip should put them in a zip-top bag.

**Weight and waste.** At roughly 8 g per wipe, a 36-count pack is a luxury for a weekend and excessive for most trips. One REI reviewer who liked the wipes still wished they came in smaller, lighter packs. The material is wood-pulp viscose, which answers an old complaint from a few critics who assumed it was plastic. "Compostable" still doesn't mean "bury it." Used wipes belong in a trash bag, not a cathole, and some owners pack all of them out. The wipes only compost in the right conditions, and a backcountry pit toilet isn't a compost bin.

## Pros & Cons

**Pros**

- Thick, tear-resistant fabric that holds up to a full-body wipe-down
- Alcohol-free, pH-balanced formula with a light scent and no reports of irritation among sensitive-skin owners
- Viscose fiber from wood pulp, not petroleum plastic
- Large enough to cover legs and torso, not just hands and face
- Works in hot, dirty conditions and in the cold

**Cons**

- 292 g for the full pack is hard to justify on a short trip
- Resealable pouch is unreliable, and unsealed wipes dry out
- Not a shower substitute; some users report a slight residual feel
- Used wipes still have to be packed out, which adds trash weight to the trip

## Who Should Buy This

These suit section hikers and thru-hikers who want one clean-up ritual per day or two. They also suit people on trips of four to ten days who want to change into dry camp clothes without a sticky body. They work for anyone who wants a stronger wipe for feet, blisters and toilet cleanup. Gram-counters on weekend trips should look at the smaller packs, or count out a handful (four wipes weigh about 32 g) and put them in a zip-top bag. Anyone looking for a single wipe that replaces a bandana wash, or who is unwilling to carry out used wipes, should look elsewhere.

## Verdict

Sea to Summit's Wilderness Wipes are the strongest and most pleasant wipes in the category, and owners rate them consistently. The pouch and the weight keep the score down. Buy the 36-pack for a long trip or to share, and decant it before you go.
