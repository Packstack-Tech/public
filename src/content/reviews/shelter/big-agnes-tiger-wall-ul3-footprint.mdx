## Overview

The Big Agnes Tiger Wall UL3 Footprint is a purpose-built ground cloth for the Tiger Wall UL3 and UL3 mtnGLO tents — specifically those equipped with TipLok Tent Buckles (post-2021 models). It serves two functions: protecting the tent's thin 20D floor from abrasion and puncture, and enabling the "Fast Fly" shelter configuration, where you pitch just the rainfly and poles over the footprint, leaving the tent body at home. It's aimed at Tiger Wall UL3 owners who either camp on rough ground regularly, want to extend the life of a serious investment, or want a lighter quick-and-dirty shelter option for fair-weather trips.

## Performance

### Floor Protection

The Tiger Wall UL3's tent floor uses 20D nylon — impressively light, but legitimately fragile. <cite index="1-5">Given how light the fabric is, reviewers consistently recommend using the tent with a footprint.</cite> That's not just marketing spin: <cite index="20-1,20-2">at least one owner called the tent "light but fragile," reporting that after a two-night trip without a footprint, there were numerous holes in the floor.</cite> The case for some kind of ground protection is real. The Big Agnes footprint handles that job competently — <cite index="37-25">users report that it fits perfectly, adds minimal weight, and provides that extra layer of protection for a high-quality tent investment.</cite>

The HyperBead™ fabric is the headline material story here. <cite index="31-6">It's recycled 15D nylon ripstop with a 1500mm waterproof silicone and polyurethane treatment, and it contains no intentionally added PFAS.</cite> <cite index="31-6">All seams are taped with waterproof, solvent-free polyurethane tape — no PVC, no VOCs.</cite> That's a legitimate sustainability credential, not just greenwashing. The silicone/PU combo also means the underside resists ground moisture from soaking through during long stationary nights — relevant when camping on damp alpine meadows or sandy desert flats.

### Fast Fly Mode

This is where the official footprint earns its keep over a generic ground cloth. <cite index="31-5">The Fast Fly setup works by staking out the footprint, inserting the tent poles into the corners, draping the fly over the top, and buckling it in using the TipLok system — giving you a lighter, smaller-packing shelter without the tent body.</cite> In practice, users and forum discussions confirm that <cite index="4-8,4-9">Fast Fly mode on Big Agnes tents means the rainfly goes over the frame and attaches to the footprint — and it's not clear how you'd manage without the footprint to anchor the fly.</cite> A Tyvek rectangle simply can't replicate this integration.

That said, compatibility is a genuine gotcha. <cite index="31-5">The footprint is fully compatible with Tiger Wall UL3 models made after 2021, and may work with older models, but without Fast Fly capability.</cite> One user who bought this as a replacement learned the hard way that <cite index="37-4,37-5">their older UL3 Platinum had different buckles and wouldn't support Fast Fly mode — the footprint still works for basic floor protection, but the TipLok-specific integration is lost on older hardware.</cite> Check your tent's manufacture date before buying.

### Weight and Packability

At 198g (7 oz), this is about as light as a purpose-cut, taped footprint for a three-person tent is going to get. Packed size of 7.5" × 5" × 1.5" means it nestles tidily alongside your poles and stakes. <cite index="7-8">It's reportedly easy to fit the body, fly, footprint, poles, and stakes all inside the provided stuff sack.</cite> I wouldn't call 7 oz negligible — on a longer thru-hike where every ounce counts, it's a conversation — but for most three-season backpackers, it's a reasonable trade for the floor protection you're getting.

## Pros & Cons

**Pros**

- Perfect fit — designed specifically for the Tiger Wall UL3 geometry
- Enables genuine Fast Fly mode, which a generic ground cloth cannot replicate
- Recycled HyperBead™ 15D fabric is light and genuinely waterproof (1500mm)
- PFAS-free materials and PVC-free seam tape — cleaner chemistry than many competitors
- Compact packed size; fits inside the tent's stuff sack with room to spare
- Waterproof, solvent-free taped seams prevent ground moisture from wicking through

**Cons**

- Sold separately from a tent that already costs several hundred dollars — a common frustration among buyers
- Full Fast Fly functionality requires a post-2021 tent with TipLok buckles; older Tiger Wall UL3 owners lose that feature
- At 7 oz, it adds meaningful weight when the tent body itself is already under 3 lbs
- Price premium is hard to justify if you only want basic floor protection — a Tyvek or polycro ground cloth does that for a fraction of the cost
- No inherent padding — this is purely a protective layer, not insulation

## Who Should Buy This

This footprint makes the most sense for Tiger Wall UL3 owners who plan to use Fast Fly mode — whether on solo fair-weather trips where you'd rather leave the tent body behind, or as a weight-saving option on long routes with predictable weather. <cite index="2-6,2-7,2-8">One multi-night user reported no issues through two days of rain, though noted the footprint should have come included with the tent</cite> — a common sentiment. If you're camping exclusively on soft, clear ground and have zero interest in Fast Fly, a Tyvek groundsheet cut to fit or a polycro sheet will protect the floor just as well for a fraction of the weight and cost. <cite index="21-1">Section Hiker, for instance, opts for a much less expensive piece of Tyvek for the same floor protection purpose.</cite> But if you want full system integration and the Fast Fly option on the menu, this is the only piece that actually does the job.

## Verdict

The Big Agnes Tiger Wall UL3 Footprint is a well-made, purpose-specific accessory that genuinely delivers on floor protection and unlocks a useful feature no generic ground cloth can match. The PFAS-free, recycled HyperBead fabric is a nice materials story, and the fit is as good as you'd expect from a brand-matched piece. The sting is the price of a separate purchase on top of an already-premium tent, and if Fast Fly isn't in your plans, a Tyvek cut-to-fit puts money back in your pocket. Buy it if you intend to use Fast Fly or if you're hard on your campsites; skip it (and grab polycro) if you just want basic floor insurance.
