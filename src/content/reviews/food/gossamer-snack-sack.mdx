## Overview

The Snack Sack is a big, flat-bottomed roll-top bag made from coated Tyvek. It weighs 37 g (1.3 oz) and is meant to hold a multi-day food supply in one place. The trade-off is the usual one for ultralight organization: it's a very light way to keep food dry and corralled, but it gives no protection from animals.

## Performance

**Capacity and organization.** At 45 cm tall and 33 cm wide, with a gusseted base, the Snack Sack is large for a food bag. A UK retailer that reviewed it found it roomy enough for a week of food, snacks included. Retailer specs put the volume at roughly 14 L. The gusset lets it sit upright in a tent vestibule or on a rock, so the top opens wide and a hand can reach the bottom without tipping the bag over. Most stuff sacks need to be dumped out for that.

**Closure.** The roll-top uses Velcro to hold the roll in place. As the food shrinks over the days, the bag rolls down with it, which keeps it compact in the pack. One retailer suggests at least three turns of the roll, so the weight of the food doesn't hang on the seams. That's good advice for any lightweight roll-top.

**Weather protection.** Tyvek sheds rain, drizzle, and condensation well, and the coating adds to that. Gossamer Gear calls the bag water resistant, not waterproof, and that is the right label. It will protect a tortilla stack from a wet pack-liner edge or a damp vestibule. It isn't a dry bag for river crossings or a bag to leave in a puddle overnight. Hikers who line their packs with a trash-compactor bag or a roll-top liner already have the real waterproofing. The Snack Sack's job is to keep food from spreading through the pack.

**Weight.** At 37 g it costs a few grams more than a plain bread bag or a trash bag and gives much more structure. Tyvek is also tougher than bag plastics, so it should stand up better to being stuffed, rolled, and pulled out of a pack many times. Tyvek is crinkly and stiff at first, and it softens with use.

**Animal protection.** Gossamer Gear is blunt about the limits: the bag is for hanging or storing food away from animals, and it is not bear proof or odor proof. Tyvek won't stop a chewing mouse, and it does nothing about smell. In places that require a hard-sided canister, it can only serve as an organizer inside one. Where a hang or an Ursack-style approach is accepted, a UK retailer notes that the small loop on the bag lets it hang off the floor and away from mice, which suits a bothy or a shelter.

**Owner sentiment.** Reviews on Garage Grown Gear average 4.6 out of 5 across 17 ratings. Fourteen of those are five-star, and the rest are one each at four, three, and two stars. A separate retailer listing shows a single verified five-star rating. The pattern is of a simple product that does what it says, with no complaints of seam failures that stand out.

## Pros & Cons

**Pros**

- 37 g (1.3 oz) for a bag that holds about a week of food
- Gusseted base stands up, so it can be packed and unpacked without a mess
- Velcro roll-top compresses as food is eaten
- Tyvek handles drizzle and damp, and it is more durable than plastic bags
- PFAS-free material

**Cons**

- Not bear proof and not odor proof, by the maker's own statement
- Water resistant only, not a submersible dry bag
- Tyvek crinkles and is noisy when new
- Too big for short trips or solo weekend food loads
- Opaque material means digging to find a specific item

## Who Should Buy This

The Snack Sack suits thru-hikers and multi-day section hikers who hang their food or carry it in places where a hang or an animal-resistant sack isn't required. It also suits anyone who wants one bag to hold a week of meals and snacks in a shape that stays open. It's a good replacement for a bag of loose zip-top food bags.

Anyone hiking where canisters are mandatory should look elsewhere for animal protection. Hikers who want a bag that can handle a canoe capsize or a submerged crossing should choose a proper welded dry bag, and weekenders may find the size excessive.

## Verdict

The Snack Sack is a well-sized, well-priced piece of food organization at 37 g, and owners rate it highly for doing a simple job. It does not protect against animals or heavy water, and Gossamer Gear says so. Buy it as an organizer and a hang bag, and not as a defense.
