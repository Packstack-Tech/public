## Overview

Real Turmat's Squash and Sweet Corn Casserole (sold in Norway as Squash- og maisgryte) is a freeze-dried, fully plant-based dinner built on rice, lentils, and coconut milk. It is a gentle, creamy counterpoint to the chili-heavy, salt-forward field of freeze-dried meals, and it is free of gluten, lactose, and every major allergen. The trade-off is a modest protein count and a salt load that some eaters notice.

## Performance

**Calorie density.** At 510 kcal for 116 g, the casserole works out to roughly 4.4 kcal per gram before water. That is solid rather than spectacular for the category, and the fat does much of the work: 20 g comes from coconut milk and rapeseed oil. The ingredient list leads with rice at 30 percent, followed by lentils at 13 percent, squash and coconut milk at about 10 percent each, then bell pepper, chickpeas, onion, and corn at 5.6 percent. It reads like something made in a kitchen, not a lab.

**Preparation.** The routine is the standard freeze-dried drill. Tear the pouch at the notch, pour hot water to the fill line (340 ml), stir, zip it shut, and wait about eight minutes. Cold-water soaking is possible with a longer wait, which suits stoveless hikers willing to plan ahead. The pouch should be treated as fresh food once opened, and the meal should be eaten within two hours of adding water. The resealable pouch doubles as the bowl, so there is nothing to wash.

**Taste and texture.** Reviewers describe a mild, slightly sweet, creamy stew in which the coconut milk rounds off the lentils and squash. Retail staff at Basecamp Gear and Ultralight Outdoor Gear both gave it 9/10 in their taste tests. One tester called it an unexpected favorite, with good consistency and a hint of spice, though they said it could take a splash more water. Buyers on a German retailer's site rate it five stars for being easy to prepare, calorie-rich, and tasty, and a verified buyer on Moory calls it one of his favorites for hiking. The most common criticism is texture: one UK reviewer reported small hard lumps that persisted even when the instructions were followed exactly. Those who find the stew light on bulk have an easy fix, since a handful of separately cooked noodles was one buyer's way of stretching the calories.

**Nutrition trade-offs.** Thirteen grams of protein is thin for a 510-kcal dinner after a high-mileage day, and it is the main reason to pair this meal with a protein-rich snack or a spoonful of nut butter. At 2.1 g of salt, it will suit hikers who sweat heavily, but one diabetic customer with high blood pressure found both the salt and the sweetness more than they wanted. The sweetness comes from the squash, corn, and a little sugar in the bouillon.

**Shelf life and storage.** A five-year shelf life from production makes it a sensible choice for resupply boxes, emergency kits, and bulk buying. It stays shelf-stable at room temperature with no refrigeration.

**Cost.** Retail prices run roughly €10–12 or £11 per pouch, with the Norwegian list price at 125 NOK. That is in line with other premium European freeze-dried dinners.

## Pros & Cons

**Pros**

- 510 kcal in 116 g, with a prepared weight of 460 g
- Mild, creamy flavor that earns consistently high taste-test scores
- Vegan, gluten-free, lactose-free, and free of declared allergens
- Real-food ingredient list, led by rice and lentils
- Ready in about eight minutes, eaten from the pouch
- Five-year shelf life

**Cons**

- Only 13 g protein per pouch
- Salt and sugar are noticeable for some eaters
- Some buyers report small hard lumps after rehydration
- Mild profile can read as bland to hikers who want heat
- Priced at the premium end for a single-serving meal

## Who Should Buy This

Vegan, gluten-free, and dairy-free hikers who are tired of dinners that lean on whey or cheese powder will find this an easy match. It also suits overnighters and weeklong trips where a comforting, kid-friendly, non-spicy dinner is welcome. High-mileage thru-hikers who need serious protein, or those who want a bold, spicy meal, should look elsewhere or plan to supplement. Anyone on a sodium-restricted diet should check the 2.1 g salt figure first.

## Verdict

Real Turmat's Squash and Sweet Corn Casserole is a well-made, easy-going vegan dinner. It delivers 510 kcal at a pack weight of 116 g and has earned warm reviews for its flavor. Buy it as a comfort dinner, add a protein source if the trip is long, and expect a mild, creamy bowl rather than a punchy one.
