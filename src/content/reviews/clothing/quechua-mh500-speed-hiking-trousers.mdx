## Overview

The Quechua MH500 is Decathlon's mid-tier mountain-walking trouser: a slim, bi-stretch synthetic pant with preformed knees, a crotch gusset and a built-in belt. It is aimed at regular hikers rather than gram-counters, and it undercuts most name-brand competitors by a wide margin. The trade-off is a fabric that stretches well and dries fast but is not the airiest option in hot weather, and a cut that runs narrow.

## Performance

**Movement.** The MH500's pitch is trousers that disappear on the body, and the construction backs it up. The 15% elastane content is high for a walking pant, and the combination of articulated knees and a crotch gusset keeps the fabric from binding on steep steps or high-stepping over deadfall. Owners who have logged serious distance in them describe the freedom of movement as the main reason they keep buying them. One Camino de Santiago walker who covered the 35-day French Way in a second pair, after owning a first pair since 2019, called them the model that ended a long search for hiking trousers.

**Fit.** The cut is slim, closer to a skinny hiking pant than a traditional straight-leg trail trouser. The same Camino walker advises sizing up for anyone with strong thighs or a preference for a looser fit, and Decathlon's own US listing for the women's version notes that it runs small. Anyone planning to layer thermals beneath for shoulder-season walking should account for that.

**Waist and hem.** The siliconized elastic waistband grips a base layer or shirt hem and stays put under a hipbelt, and the integrated belt lets the waist be dialed in without a separate accessory. The belt can be detached. A drawstring at the ankle cinches the leg to keep brush and snow off the boots, a small feature that many more expensive trousers omit.

**Breathability and drying.** The fabric is synthetic polyamide, so it wicks sweat and dries quickly after a wetting from rain or a creek crossing. Breathability is middling. Decathlon's Spanish site rates it 3 out of 5, and owner experience matches: it ventilates adequately when moving in warm weather, but it is not a featherweight summer fabric. Hikers heading into sustained heat will find a lighter, looser pant or a pair of shorts more comfortable.

**Pockets.** Every pocket zips. That is useful for a phone or permit, and it means nothing bounces out on a scramble. The men's version has four zip pockets; the women's has two hand pockets and a thigh pocket.

**Durability and weight.** The 300 g weight is respectable for a full-coverage stretch pant but not ultralight territory, and the stretchy, thin face fabric is not built for bushwhacking or prolonged abrasion. Not every owner is convinced at distance: one verified Decathlon buyer called them better suited to forest walks than long hikes, while repeat purchasers praise the fabric's lightness and elasticity. The two-year warranty adds some comfort at this price.

## Pros & Cons

**Pros**
- Very low price: £29.99 in the UK for the men's cut
- High stretch (15% elastane) with preformed knees and a crotch gusset
- Fast-drying synthetic fabric
- Integrated adjustable belt and ankle drawstring
- All-zip pockets
- Machine washable, with a two-year warranty

**Cons**
- Slim cut; many owners size up
- Moderate breathability, rated 3/5 by Decathlon
- At 300 g, not especially light for a fair-weather pant
- Stretchy fabric is a questionable match for off-trail abrasion
- At least one buyer found it better suited to short walks than long hikes

## Who Should Buy This

Budget-minded day hikers and section hikers who want a no-fuss, stretchy trouser for cool-to-mild conditions will get plenty from the MH500, as will anyone who needs a second pair for camp or travel. It also suits long-distance walkers on maintained routes such as the Camino. Hikers in hot, humid climates, anyone shaving their kit to a strict base weight, and those who regularly push through thorny scrub should look at lighter, looser or more abrasion-resistant trousers.

## Verdict

The MH500 offers more stretch and thoughtful hiking detailing than its price suggests, and it has earned repeat buyers who walk long distances in it. Size up if there is any doubt, and keep it out of the hottest conditions. For the money, it is a strong buy.
