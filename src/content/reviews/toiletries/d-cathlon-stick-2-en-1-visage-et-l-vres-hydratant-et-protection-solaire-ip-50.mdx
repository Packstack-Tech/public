## Overview

Décathlon's 2-in-1 Face & Lip Sunscreen Stick packs SPF 50+ broad-spectrum protection into a 9.5 g block of wax — small enough to disappear into a hip-belt pocket and light enough that you'll forget it's there until you need it. <cite index="2-1,2-2,2-3">A lip balm is integrated directly into the cap, and the formula carries a full SPF 50+ rating with very high UVA/UVB protection.</cite> It's aimed squarely at outdoor athletes — hikers, ski tourers, trail runners — who want a single item to cover both face and lips without fussing with two products or getting sunscreen on their gloves.

## Performance

**Protection level.** <cite index="2-8">SPF 50+ delivers very high UVA-UVB protection and the sweat-resistant formula is built to hold up during both leisure and intensive sport activities, including snow with its high UV reflectivity.</cite> The UV filter system is worth noting: <cite index="2-7">the full ingredient list includes Diethylamino Hydroxybenzoyl Hexyl Benzoate, Diethylhexyl Butamido Triazone, Bis-Ethylhexyloxyphenol Methoxyphenyl Triazine, Ethylhexyl Triazone, and Titanium Dioxide</cite> — a mix of next-generation chemical filters common in European formulations and a small dose of mineral titanium dioxide. These are photostable filters not yet approved for the US market, so if you're used to American sunscreens, this formula is a step forward in UV coverage.

**Application and on-skin feel.** <cite index="22-2">You can apply it without getting your hands dirty</cite>, which matters on a cold morning when you'd rather not strip off gloves or have greasy sunscreen on your trekking pole grips. <cite index="7-11">It was specifically designed to let you protect your face and lips without having to remove gloves during outdoor activities.</cite> The wax base — <cite index="19-4,19-5">built on a sunflower seed wax with a high melting point that provides excellent thermal stability and keeps stick-type formulas solid</cite> — means it should hold its shape in warm conditions better than softer balms, though I'd still keep it out of a direct-sun dashboard situation in summer.

**The 2-in-1 design.** The lip balm tucked into the cap is genuinely smart on paper: one item does two jobs, and you don't have to rummage for a separate ChapStick. In practice, the smaller cap-integrated stick means you're dealing with a very short, stubby applicator for the lips — functional, but less precise than a dedicated lip balm tube. The upside is the face stick itself has a broader, flatter profile that covers the nose, cheeks, and forehead efficiently with a few swipes.

**Ingredients and skin compatibility.** <cite index="2-19">As of the 2022/23 winter update, the formulation now contains 77% ingredients of natural origin according to ISO 16128</cite>, which is a meaningful improvement. <cite index="19-26,19-27">Shea butter extract — a triterpene ester-rich fraction — is included for its claimed barrier-strengthening and anti-inflammatory properties.</cite> One ingredient worth flagging: <cite index="19-29,19-30,19-31">fragrance is present in the formula, which is a concern for anyone who prefers to know exactly what's on their skin, and it's the number one cause of contact allergy in cosmetics.</cite> If you have reactive or sensitive skin, this is worth watching.

**Environmental credentials.** <cite index="22-14,22-15">The formula has been tested by an independent laboratory for ecotoxicity on algae and corals in accordance with ISO 10253 / ISO 8692, with results showing no negative impact on marine flora and fauna under normal use conditions.</cite> <cite index="22-31,22-32">Décathlon also goes beyond international cosmetic regulations, banning more than 1,000 controversial substances including recognised endocrine disruptors and certain preservatives.</cite> Hawaii law compliance (no oxybenzone or octinoxate) is confirmed.

**Coverage and longevity.** 9.5 g is genuinely light, but it also means the stick won't last indefinitely on a long trip if you're reapplying properly every two hours. <cite index="22-25">Generous, even application prior to sun exposure with reapplication at least every two hours is recommended.</cite> For a week-long trip with daily full-face coverage, budget to bring two sticks or supplement with a tube sunscreen for the body.

## Pros & Cons

**Pros**

- 9.5 g all-in — one of the lightest face+lip sunscreen options available
- Lip balm integrated into cap: zero extra weight for lip coverage
- Next-generation European UV filter system with true broad-spectrum coverage
- Sweat-resistant and tested in high-reflection snow conditions
- 77% natural-origin ingredients, reef-safe, Hawaii law compliant
- Dermatologically tested; no oxybenzone or octinoxate
- No-hands-dirty stick application — works fine with gloves on

**Cons**

- Contains fragrance (Parfum) — a potential irritant for sensitive skin
- Beeswax-based formula: not vegan-friendly
- 9.5 g goes fast with disciplined reapplication — carry a backup on multi-week trips
- Cap-integrated lip balm is a clever design, but losing the cap means losing the lip balm
- Limited independent English-language user reviews to draw on; hard to verify manufacturer sweat-resistance claims through community testing
- Stick format can apply unevenly in cold temperatures when the wax stiffens

## Who Should Buy This

This is a natural fit for gram-counting backpackers, trail runners, and ski tourers who want a single item to cover face and lips without the extra weight of two products. It performs best for three-season use in moderate to high UV environments — mountain ridgelines, open snowfields, exposed desert trails — and the no-mess stick format makes it easy to reapply mid-activity. If you have fragrance-sensitive skin, look elsewhere (Badger Mineral SPF 35 Face Stick is fragrance-free and reef-safe, though heavier at ~17 g). If you're vegan, the beeswax base is a dealbreaker.

## Verdict

At 9.5 g with an SPF 50+ rating, a photostable EU filter stack, and a lip balm literally built into the cap, this stick is a hard combination to beat for weight-conscious hikers. The fragrance inclusion and small net content are real trade-offs, but neither is a trip-ender. For the casual to serious day-hiker or thru-hiker who's been carrying a separate face stick and lip balm, consolidating to this one item is an easy win on both weight and pack organization. **7.5/10.**
