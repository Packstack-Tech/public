## Overview

The TOAKS Titanium 550ml Bowl is a wide, shallow, uncoated titanium dish with folding handles and a mesh stuff sack. It is built for hikers who already carry, or plan to carry, a TOAKS 900 or 1100 ml pot and want a lightweight eating vessel that stores inside it. The trade-off is simple: it does one job well and a second job passably, and it has no lid to help with anything else.

## Performance

**Eating.** The 118 mm opening is the bowl's main argument. Most titanium "cups" are tall and narrow, which makes a spoon scrape the bottom at an awkward angle and leaves granola or couscous stranded in the corners. At just 50 mm deep, this one behaves like an actual bowl. A full 550 ml serving of oatmeal, instant mashed potatoes or a cold-soak dinner sits low and wide, and the rolled rim gives the thin wall some extra stiffness and a smoother edge against the lips.

**Cooking.** The folding handles make the bowl usable over a flame, and TOAKS positions it as a "cooking tool," but the geometry points it toward gentle jobs: warming leftovers, melting butter or chocolate, heating a small portion. Thin-walled titanium conducts heat unevenly. Reviewers of TOAKS' titanium pots consistently report hot spots, limited simmer control and handles that get hot over a stove, and a shallow bowl with a wide base magnifies the first problem. Treat it as a vessel for food that is already mostly cooked, not as a primary pot.

**Double-boiler use.** This is the distinguishing feature. The bowl rests snugly on top of a 900 or 1100 ml TOAKS pot, so steam from boiling water below heats the food above. For a hiker cooking eggs, reheating a dehydrated stew or avoiding scorched cheese sauce, that adds a gentler technique to a kit that usually offers only a rolling boil. It is also a legitimate way to dodge the burnt-on residue that plagues thin titanium. It works only with the larger TOAKS pots, though; a 750 ml pot is too narrow for it.

**Packing.** The same 118 mm bowl drops inside the 900 and 1100 ml TOAKS pots, so the bowl effectively costs no pack volume in a nested kit. The folding handles lie flat, and the mesh sack keeps it from rattling against a stove or fuel canister. The sack also doubles as a handy scrubber, a trick TOAKS owners often mention for the brand's cookware.

**Weight.** TOAKS lists the bowl at 62 g. Some retailer listings still carry a 78 g (2.7 oz) figure, so weight-obsessed buyers should put it on a scale. Either way, it adds less than a hiker's spork and a spare lighter combined, but it is not free. Anyone who eats straight from the cook pot saves the whole weight.

**Durability.** Titanium does not rust, does not hold odors and does not need a coating, which TOAKS does not apply. Dents are the realistic failure mode, and the rolled edge helps. The folding handle hinges are the parts most likely to loosen with years of use, as they are on any folding-handle design.

## Pros & Cons

**Pros**

- 62 g, uncoated titanium with no coating to flake or off-gas
- Wide, shallow shape is comfortable to eat from and easy to clean
- Nests in the TOAKS 900 and 1100 ml pots and rests on top as a double boiler
- Folding handles and mesh sack with a locking slider included
- Rolled rim adds stiffness and a smoother lip feel
- Titanium is corrosion-resistant and odor-free

**Cons**

- No lid, so heat retention and meal rehydration are weak
- Thin walls produce hot spots and hot handles over a flame
- Double-boiler trick works only with specific TOAKS pots
- Priced at $26.95 for a bowl that does not cook as a pot does
- Capacity is measured to the brim, so the practical fill is lower

## Who Should Buy This

This bowl suits hikers who own a TOAKS 900 or 1100 ml pot and want a proper eating dish that adds no pack volume, as well as pairs who cook once and eat from separate vessels. It also suits anyone who wants to try gentler double-boiler cooking on the trail. Solo hikers who cook by boiling water and rehydrating in a bag, or who eat from a pot with a lid, gain little from it. Likewise, anyone on a smaller TOAKS 750 ml kit should look at the narrower 103 mm bowl, which fits that pot family.

## Verdict

The TOAKS Titanium 550ml Bowl is a small, well-matched accessory: light, durable and sized to disappear inside the pots it is designed for. Buy it as part of a TOAKS nested system. Skip it if a single lidded pot already covers the job.
