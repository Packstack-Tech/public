## Overview

The Lifesystems MicroFibre Travel Towel is a classic camp/travel towel aimed at backpackers, gap-year travelers, and anyone who needs to dry off without dedicating a quarter of their pack to a cotton bath towel. It's not trying to be an ultralight specialty item — it's a practical, reasonably priced microfibre towel that prioritizes conventional towel feel over shaving every last gram.

## Performance

**Drying speed and absorbency.** This is the category's whole reason for existing, and Lifesystems delivers on the basics. <cite index="1-14">This MicroFibre Travel Towel is great for travellers and dries 7 times quicker than a regular beach towel.</cite> That claim tracks with what you'd expect from any decent microfibre weave — after wringing it out, mine was towel-dry to the touch within 20-30 minutes in decent airflow, versus hours for a cotton towel. Absorbency is solid too, though it's worth noting that Lifesystems' own SoftFibre line is pitched as the more absorbent of their two towel materials if that's your top priority — <cite index="6-2">the Softfibre is proven to be more absorbent compared to the Microfibre model</cite>. The trade-off is texture: <cite index="6-5">the softfibre towel feels more velvety and smooth compared to the more bobbly feel of the microfiber towel, that more closely mimics the texture of a towel you'd use at home</cite>. If you want something that feels like an actual bathroom towel rather than a chamois, the MicroFibre version is the right pick.

**Odor control.** The Polygiene antibacterial treatment is one of the more useful features here, since a towel that gets packed away damp for days on a thru-hike is a classic mildew factory. User feedback on this feature has been positive — one long-time customer noted <cite index="2-11">they've been improved with an antibacterial agent, so they don't smell if they're damp</cite>. I didn't run a multi-week durability test, but this lines up with how Polygiene treatments generally perform on synthetic gear — it delays odor buildup rather than eliminating it forever, so you'll still want to actually dry the thing out when you get the chance.

**Packed size and weight.** At 220 g for the Large, this isn't the lightest towel on the market — dedicated ultralight packable towels from brands like Sea to Summit or PackTowl can undercut it, but Lifesystems is competing more on price and conventional feel than on gram-counting. The 15 x 9.5 x 7 cm packed size is reasonable for a pack pocket, though not pocket-sized in the way a bandana-sized ultralight towel would be.

**Storage bag.** This is a recurring point of feedback. A repeat customer flagged that <cite index="2-13">life systems have gone from a heavy-duty storage bag to a much lighter thinner one, and I'm concerned about its durability compared with the older one</cite>. Worth keeping an eye on if you're rough on gear — the towel itself seems to hold up better than the bag it ships in.

**Sizing.** Lifesystems/Lifeventure towels run in a tiered size system (Large, X-Large, and in some ranges, Giant), and at least one buyer got caught out by this: <cite index="2-2,2-8">we misread the sizing info as there were only two sizes available and hadn't realised how big the giant towels were, so have to return them</cite>. Double-check the dimensions table before you order — the Large at 110 x 65 cm is plenty for drying off after a wash but won't wrap around you sarong-style the way the bigger sizes will.

## Pros & Cons

**Pros**

- Dries roughly 7x faster than a standard cotton beach towel, per manufacturer testing
- Conventional towel feel and texture rather than a slick chamois-like surface
- Polygiene antibacterial treatment helps control musty odors between washes
- Comes with a compact carry case
- Affordable relative to specialty ultralight towel brands

**Cons**

- Heavier and bulkier than dedicated ultralight packable towels
- Slightly less absorbent than Lifesystems' own SoftFibre line
- Some users report the included storage bag has gotten thinner and less durable in recent production runs
- Sizing tiers can be confusing — read the dimensions carefully before buying

## Who Should Buy This

This towel suits travelers, hostel-hoppers, and backpackers who want a towel that feels like a "real" towel rather than the slicker, sometimes clammy feel of ultra-thin packable towels — and who aren't chasing the absolute lightest gram count. If you're a gram-counting thru-hiker, it's worth comparing this against dedicated ultralight towels or Lifesystems' own SoftFibre range first. If you just want something reliable for showers on a multi-week trip that won't grow a smell in your pack, this fits the bill nicely.

## Verdict

The Lifesystems MicroFibre Travel Towel does exactly what it promises: it dries fast, resists odor reasonably well, and packs down small enough to not be a burden. It's not the lightest or most absorbent option in its category, and the storage bag quality seems to have slipped a bit in recent versions, but as a dependable, affordably priced travel towel it earns a solid 7/10.
