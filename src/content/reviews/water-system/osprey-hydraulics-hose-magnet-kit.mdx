## Overview

The Hydraulics Hose Magnet Kit is a two-piece magnetic dock for a hydration reservoir's bite valve: one half slides onto the hose, the other mounts on a pack's sternum strap. It is aimed at anyone who has watched a drinking tube swing loose on a descent or vanish behind a shoulder strap. The trade-off is a small, loose part that has to stay put on the strap, in exchange for a hose that snaps back to the same spot every time.

## Performance

**Retention and reach.** The appeal is the click. Drop the bite valve near the chest and the magnets pull it into place, so the hose stops flapping and the mouthpiece is always where a hand expects it. Owners describe the magnet as strong, and a European retailer's reviewers say it holds the hose in place even on rough terrain. One buyer who tried it with a Platypus hose and another who used it on a Camelbak both reported that it worked, though the kit is built around 0.25 in hoses and Osprey's own reservoirs.

**Adjustability.** The hose-side piece slides along the tube, which lets users set the bite valve height instead of accepting a fixed position. Reviewers single this out as a plus, and it is the main reason owners prefer it to simple spring clips. One reviewer said the sternum-mounted design beats the basic clip-on types that fall off too often.

**Installation.** This is where complaints cluster. An REI reviewer who still gave five stars said feeding the hose through the hole in the magnet attachment was very hard. Another found the redesigned mouthpiece diameter differed from the older Osprey version, so mixing old and new parts did not give a good seal. A Bergfreunde reviewer could not fit the Osprey clip around a neoprene insulating sleeve and went back to a different hose clip. Anyone running an insulated hose for cold weather should plan around that.

**Strap fit and durability.** The strap-side mount is the weak point in the system. Australian Hiker, which rated the kit 8.9 out of 10, notes it fits narrow sternum straps only and won't suit a wide one. A reviewer on Dick's Sporting Goods reported the strap mount would not stay affixed and said they had lost two. The magnet also picks up grit and trail debris; Australian Hiker's tester wipes it down daily on long hikes, which is a small chore but an honest one.

**Safety.** The magnet is strong enough that REI shoppers flag the manufacturer's warnings. One reviewer pointed out that magnets near a pacemaker or ICD are a concern. Users with implanted medical devices should check with their physician before wearing a magnet on the chest.

**Weight.** At 9 g, it is a rounding error in a base weight. It also replaces the plastic hose clip most reservoirs already ship with, so the net penalty is smaller still.

## Pros & Cons

**Pros**

- 9 g, with a strong magnet that returns the bite valve to the same spot every time
- Hose piece slides to dial in bite valve height
- Stops hose flap on rough ground
- Works with other brands' 0.25 in hoses, according to owners
- Strong buyer ratings: 4.6 out of 5 across 26 reviews on REI

**Cons**

- Strap mount fits narrow sternum straps only
- Some owners report the mount slipping off and getting lost
- Threading the hose through the magnet piece can be a fight
- Doesn't fit over neoprene hose insulation
- Magnet collects debris and needs regular wiping
- Magnet warning for people with pacemakers or ICDs
- Many Osprey reservoirs already include one

## Who Should Buy This

Hikers who run a hydration reservoir with a 0.25 in hose and a pack with a narrow sternum strap will get the most from it, especially those replacing a lost or worn Osprey magnet or retrofitting a non-magnetic bladder from another brand. It suits day hikers, trail runners and thru-hikers who want to drink without stopping or using two hands.

Skip it if the pack has a wide sternum strap, if the hose wears an insulating sleeve, or if the reservoir is an Osprey that already includes the magnet. Bottle-pocket users have no use for it. Anyone with a pacemaker or similar implant should look at a non-magnetic clip.

## Verdict

The Hose Magnet Kit does one small job well: it keeps a bite valve where it belongs for 9 g. The narrow-strap requirement and reports of the strap piece slipping off keep it from a higher score, so check the sternum strap width before ordering and buy a spare mount if the first one goes missing.
