## Overview

Decathlon's Men's Pack of 3 Breathable Boxers is a bundle of synthetic, stretch-microfibre boxer shorts from its Kalenji running line, built to move sweat off the skin and dry quickly. They are aimed at runners and everyday wearers, but the same traits (fast drying, offset seams, no cotton) make them a natural fit for long-distance hikers who want underwear that survives a week of sweat and a sink wash. The trade-off is a boxer cut that is looser than a brief or boxer-brief, which suits some hikers and annoys others.

## Performance

**Moisture management.** The core pitch is simple: a synthetic fabric that pulls perspiration away from the skin and dries faster than cotton. Decathlon rates the boxers as breathable and suitable for high-intensity use, and the closely related single-pair Kalenji boxer lists a polyester-and-elastane blend of 84 percent and 16 percent. On the trail, that means a pair rinsed in a stream or a hostel sink in the evening has a fair chance of being wearable by morning, which is the entire argument for synthetic underwear on a thru-hike.

**Chafe control.** Decathlon moved the seams out of the crotch area to cut down on rubbing, and the long cut gives the legs room to swing without the fabric bunching at the groin. The waistband is an elastic band described as holding without clamping down on the waist. Runners in the broader Kalenji boxer range rate the line highly: the single-pair version holds a 4.7 average across more than 17,000 reviews on Decathlon's India site, while the UK listing for this three-pack sits at 4.4.

**Durability.** Long-term evidence points the same way. One walker on the Camino de Santiago forum described a pair of Kalenji boxers still going years after a long pilgrimage, now semi-retired as swim-short liners, and praised them as light, quick-drying, supportive and comfortable. Decathlon says the pack is designed for intensive sports use and repeated washing.

**Care matters.** The fabric's wicking depends on how it is washed. Decathlon says to use liquid detergent rather than powder, skip softener entirely because it can ruin breathability, and avoid the tumble dryer. None of that is a burden for a hiker, who is more likely to be washing by hand and air-drying on a pack strap anyway.

**Limits.** This is running underwear, not a purpose-built backcountry garment. There is no merino odor resistance, so synthetic funk builds faster over multi-day stretches, and the loose boxer leg can ride up or bunch under snug shorts or a hip belt. Hikers who prefer a close, no-flap fit under running shorts will do better with a boxer-brief.

## Pros & Cons

**Pros**

- Synthetic microfibre with elastane dries quickly and wicks sweat
- Offset seams keep stitching out of the crotch to reduce chafing
- Long leg cut and stretch fabric allow free movement
- Three pairs at a budget price, enough to rotate on a section hike
- Wide customer approval across the Kalenji boxer line, with reports of long service life
- Machine washable with simple care rules

**Cons**

- Boxer cut is loose and can bunch under close-fitting shorts
- Synthetic fabric holds odor more readily than merino on long trips
- Fabric softener and tumble drying are off limits
- Standard fit only, with no snug or short-leg alternative in this pack
- Colorway choices are limited to two multi-packs

## Who Should Buy This

Runners, day hikers and budget-minded thru-hikers who want dependable synthetic underwear in a roomy cut, and who would rather buy a three-pack for the price of one pair of branded merino. They also suit anyone who wants cheap spares for travel or camp use. Hikers who need a snug, no-bunching fit under running shorts, or who go many days between washes and want the odor resistance of merino, should look at boxer-briefs or wool-blend options instead.

## Verdict

These are unfussy, quick-drying boxers that do the one job asked of them at a low price, with chafe-minded construction and a record of lasting. A loose cut and the usual synthetic smell are the compromises. For three pairs of trail-ready underwear, they are an easy recommendation for anyone who likes a boxer leg.
