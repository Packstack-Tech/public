## Overview

The Air Head is Therm-a-Rest's baseline inflatable pillow: a 4-inch baffled air bladder wrapped in a brushed polyester cover with a layer of eraLoft synthetic insulation on top. It is cut to nestle into a mummy bag hood and aimed at backpackers who want something softer than a bare air bladder without carrying foam. The trade-off is weight and bladder durability. At 159 g it sits well above the ultralight inflatables, and reviewers have flagged the air chamber as its weak point.

## Performance

**Comfort.** The pitch for the Air Head is that the synthetic-filled top takes the edge off an air pillow's usual firmness. Therm-a-Rest uses the same eraLoft insulation found in its synthetic sleeping bags, and the brushed cover is soft against the cheek. Owners consistently single out that soft outer layer and the low-fuss, lightweight feel as reasons they like it. Four inches of loft is enough for most back and side sleepers, and the baffled core keeps the surface stable rather than ballooning in the middle.

The shape is a quiet strength. The pillow is cut to sit inside a sleeping bag hood, and buyer feedback points to a good fit across a range of bags. The nonslip underside helps it stay put, a real advantage over slick-bottomed rivals that migrate off the pad at 3 a.m.

**Durability.** This is where the Air Head draws criticism. Switchback Travel's testers dismissed it, citing an internal bladder on the thin side and reports of punctures and premature leaks. They also called the plastic construction crinkly and noisy, and advised restless or light sleepers to look elsewhere. Gear reviewers of the down-topped sibling, which uses the same bladder design, have also noted a twist valve that sticks out and is slow to top off.

**Weight and packability.** At 5.6 oz (159 g) in the Regular size, the Air Head is not an ultralight pick. Therm-a-Rest's own down-topped version weighs 4.9 oz, and its Air Head Lite weighs about 2 oz. That is a gap of more than 100 g against the Lite, and on a gram-counting thru-hike it is hard to justify unless the soft top makes a difference to sleep. The pillow does deflate and stow small, and a removable machine-washable cover is a practical plus on a long trail.

**Against the competition.** Hybrid air-and-foam pillows like the NEMO Fillo are now the benchmark for comfort-per-gram, and testers at Outdoor Gear Lab and CleverHiker rank them above most pure air designs. The Air Head's synthetic top is a half-step toward that comfort, not a full one.

## Choosing a Version

The two sizes share the same 4-inch thickness, materials and construction, so the choice comes down to surface area.

- **Regular (15.5 x 11 in, 5.6 oz / 159 g):** The right size for nearly everyone. It fits a standard mummy hood and keeps the weight as low as this design allows.
- **Large (18 x 12.5 in, 7.7 oz / 218 g):** Adds about 2.1 oz and a noticeably bigger sleeping surface. It suits broad-shouldered side sleepers, restless sleepers who roll off a smaller pillow, and anyone using a roomy bag or quilt with a wide hood. Weight-conscious hikers should skip it.

## Pros & Cons

**Pros**

- Synthetic top and brushed cover soften the feel of an air bladder
- Shaped to sit in a sleeping bag hood
- Nonslip underside keeps it in place
- Removable, machine-washable cover
- Synthetic fill is less fussy about moisture than down
- Made in the USA, with Regular and Large sizes

**Cons**

- 159 g is heavy next to ultralight inflatables
- Thin bladder has drawn reports of punctures and leaks
- Crinkly, noisy bladder can bother light sleepers
- Not as plush as hybrid air-and-foam pillows
- List price of $54.95 is steep for the weight

## Who Should Buy This

The Air Head suits three-season backpackers who carry a mummy bag, want a soft head surface and don't mind spending 5–6 oz on sleep quality. It also works for weekend trips and travel, where a leak is an inconvenience, not a trip-ender. Thru-hikers watching base weight, noisy-pillow-intolerant sleepers and anyone who needs a pillow they can trust for months should look at the NEMO Fillo or at Therm-a-Rest's own Air Head Lite.

## Verdict

The Air Head is a comfortable, well-fitted pillow that is outclassed on weight and, by reviewer accounts, on bladder toughness. It is a reasonable buy for casual backpackers who prize softness, but ultralighters and long-distance hikers can do better for less weight.
