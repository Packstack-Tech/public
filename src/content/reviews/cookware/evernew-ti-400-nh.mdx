## Overview

The Ti 400 NH is Evernew's 400 ml Ti 400FD cup with the handle removed. The change drops 16 g and leaves a 34 g slab of Japanese-made pure titanium with a measurement scale inside. It suits solo hikers who boil a single mug of water and rehydrate meals, and it costs you the convenience of a handle.

## Performance

**Weight and the handle trade.** Evernew's own pitch is simple: take the Ti 400FD, delete the handle, and save 16 g. Evernew lists the NH at 34 g, so the handle accounted for roughly a third of the original cup's weight. For gram-counters, that's a real cut for a one-line edit to the design. The cost is that a single-wall titanium cup gets hot all the way up the side when it holds boiling water. Evernew's answer is a separate flame-retardant silicone band, the Flameproof Sil.RING M, which is sold on its own. With the band fitted, the cup is comfortable to hold. Without it, expect to reach for a bandana or buff.

**Shape.** At 102 mm across and 58 mm deep, the cup is wide and shallow, not tall and narrow. Owners of the handled 400FD, the same body, like that geometry. They report that it sits stably on a stove. They also say it's easier to eat from than a narrow mug and that it suits the way most solo hikers use a cup, which is to boil water, pour it into a food bag or eat directly from it. The inside scale lets you measure water for a freeze-dried meal without a separate measuring cup. That scale is the feature owners of unmarked titanium mugs most often say they wish they had.

**Fit and nesting.** The 400FD is a popular swap for the plastic base cup that ships with Jetboil Zip and Sol systems, and the handleless version's cleaner profile should suit that use even better. Owners of the 400FD say it nests inside an MSR Titan Kettle. Evernew's own listing shows it stacking with the company's deeper titanium cookers, and it was designed to ride inside them. The 400FD's rim takes Snow Peak Hot Lips silicone lip guards, which helps for drinking from a metal cup.

**Durability and heat.** Titanium means no coating to scratch off and no taste. It also means the thin wall transfers heat quickly, which is why this cup heats water fast but cools coffee fast, too. Owners who want a drink to stay hot should look at a double-wall mug instead. At this weight, titanium is also easy to dent. One 400FD owner posted a photo of a bottom dent on a brand-new cup, so a pouch or a nested position inside another pot is sensible protection.

**Limits.** The cup has no lid and no handle, so it's a boiler, not a kitchen. Four hundred milliliters covers one freeze-dried meal or a cup of ramen, and it's too small for two people or for anything that needs stirring and simmering. Evernew describes the 400 series as its smallest cooker, intended for solo use.

## Pros & Cons

**Pros**

- 34 g, about 16 g lighter than the handled Ti 400FD
- Wide, stable 102 mm footprint that's easy to eat from
- Internal measurement scale
- Pure titanium, made in Japan
- Fits Jetboil-style bases and nests with larger pots

**Cons**

- Rim and walls get too hot to hold bare-handed when full of boiling water
- The silicone ring that fixes this is a separate purchase
- No lid
- 400 ml is enough for one person and nothing more
- Single wall means drinks cool quickly, and thin titanium can dent

## Who Should Buy This

The ideal buyer is a solo hiker with a canister or alcohol stove who boils water for freeze-dried meals, instant oatmeal and coffee. It's also a good fit for someone who already owns a pot with a handle or bail and wants a nesting cup. It's a natural base cup for a Jetboil-style setup. Anyone who cooks real meals, shares a pot with a partner or wants a cup that can be lifted off the stove with bare fingers should buy the handled Ti 400FD instead, which costs 16 g and removes the hot-rim problem.

## Verdict

The Ti 400 NH is a well-made, very light cup that does one thing, boil a single serving, and does it with fewer grams than almost anything with a scale on the inside. Buy it if you plan to use a silicone ring, a cozy or a bandana and want the weight savings. Everyone else will be happier with the handle.
