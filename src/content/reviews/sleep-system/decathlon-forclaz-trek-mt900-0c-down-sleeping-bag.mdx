## Overview

The Forclaz Trek MT900 0°C is Decathlon's top-tier three-season down bag: an 800-fill mummy with an anatomical hood, a shaped footbox and a rated comfort temperature of 0°C. It is aimed at trekkers and bivouackers who want real down performance without boutique pricing. The trade-off is weight and bulk. At 930 g it carries a few ounces more than the cottage-brand bags it undercuts on price.

## Performance

**Warmth.** Reviewers who have slept in Forclaz's MT900-series bags describe warmth that outperforms the price tag, with the best results in shoulder-season conditions. Backpacker's testers said the bag does not behave like a budget pick, and one tester reported sleeping comfortably in it on an insulated Exped mat. The 0°C figure is a comfort rating, and the -5°C lower limit is the point where a sleeper is coping rather than comfortable. Thru-hikers choosing a bag for nights near freezing should treat 0°C as the working floor and add a liner or dry insulating layers if they run cold.

The 260 g fill is modest for a 0°C bag, which explains both the weight and the pack size. The loft comes from 800 cuin down, a figure usually seen in more expensive bags. The split construction, with duck down on top and recycled down on the underside, is a sensible cost-saving choice. The underside is compressed under the sleeper anyway, so the top carries most of the insulating work.

**Weight and packed size.** The bag weighs 875 g without its sack and 930 g as listed, and it stuffs to roughly 11 L. That is compact enough for a 40–50 L pack but larger than the packed volume of premium 900+ fill bags. Against synthetic bags and generic down at this price it is competitive. Against sub-two-pound bags from cottage makers it trails by a few ounces, and anyone counting grams will notice.

**Fit and features.** The hood is the bag's best feature. Reviewers of Forclaz's alpine-cut bags praise the fitted hood for sealing snugly around the head, and the anatomical shaping holds heat well on cold nights. The cut is snug, so side sleepers and broad-shouldered users should expect less room than in a roomier rectangular or semi-rectangular bag. The YKK two-way zip lets a sleeper vent from the foot end on warmer nights. A Tentlife reviewer found it easy to work in the dark, and the anti-jam system addresses the most common failure of cheap down-bag zippers: fabric caught in the teeth at 2 a.m.

**Durability.** Decathlon's down bags use a sturdier shell than elite ultralight designs. A review of the current 0°C MT900 describes a 20D polyamide face fabric. That resists abrasion and snags better than the 7D–10D shells on premium bags, which matters for bivouacking and for anyone who treats gear roughly. Owners generally describe it as a bag that tolerates abuse without constant babying. The usual down rules still apply: store it loose rather than compressed between trips, and keep it dry.

## Pros & Cons

**Pros**

- 800 cuin down at roughly half the price of comparable boutique bags
- Tougher 20D-class shell than most ultralight down bags
- Well-shaped hood and footbox that hold heat
- YKK two-way zip with anti-jam system
- Recycled down on the underside reduces cost without much effect on warmth

**Cons**

- Heavier than cottage-brand bags with a similar rating
- 11 L packed volume is larger than premium 900+ fill bags
- Snug mummy cut leaves little room to move
- 0°C is a comfort rating, so cold sleepers need to treat it conservatively

## Who Should Buy This

The MT900 suits three-season hikers, bikepackers and bivouackers who want a proper down bag at an accessible price, especially those who sleep in a bivy or on rough ground and want a shell that tolerates it. It is also a sound first down bag for someone moving up from synthetic. Gram-counting thru-hikers who want the lowest possible base weight, and side sleepers who need space, should look at a quilt or a roomier cottage-brand bag. Anyone expecting regular sub-zero nights should buy a bag with a lower comfort rating rather than count on the -5°C limit.

## Verdict

The Forclaz Trek MT900 0°C is a warm, durable and well-featured down bag that sells for far less than its specification suggests. It gives up a few ounces and some packed volume to the premium competition, and most three-season hikers will accept that for the savings. It earns a recommendation as the best-value 0°C down bag for hikers who are not chasing the lowest possible weight.
