## Overview

The Sunday Performance Jogger is Vuori's best-selling men's jogger: a midweight, 4-way stretch polyester-elastane knit cut in an athletic slim fit with a tapered leg. It has become a default pick for the gym, the airport and the couch, and it has a credible second life as camp and town-stop pants. The trade-off for backpackers is that it is a lifestyle garment first, with a $110 list price and a knit that has no obvious ambitions on rough trail.

## Performance

**Comfort and stretch.** This is the jogger's calling card. In Outside's two-month test of men's joggers, which covered gym sessions, trail runs and 20 days of coastal camping, the Sunday took the best-overall slot. The testers praised its mix of workout-grade stretch and lounge-level softness, and said the athletic slim cut stayed close to the body through squats, step-ups and box jumps. Nordstrom shoppers describe a similar balance of give and structure. Vuori's own review base runs to nearly 15,000 ratings and averages 4.8 out of 5.

**Weight and warmth.** At roughly 280 g in a medium, the Sunday is a substantial pair of pants by ultralight standards. It is not a winter layer, either: reviewers note the fabric is not heavy enough for cold-weather wear on its own. The sweet spot is spring, summer and fall, where the midweight knit works as sleep pants, camp pants or a town-day change of clothes. The retailer spec sheet lists moisture-wicking and quick-drying fabric, and owners report it dries quickly after sweating. A thicker knit than a nylon hiking pant will still hold water longer in a downpour.

**Pockets.** The front leg zip pockets and the zippered back pocket do real work. One Nordstrom reviewer said the back pocket passed the "wallet test", and others single out the thigh pockets as useful for travel and the gym. For a hiker, they keep a phone, key or card from escaping, though the knit isn't the fabric you want a heavily loaded pocket pulling on.

**Fit and sizing.** The 28-inch inseam is deliberately short, so the cuff hits at the ankle for people who wear 30-inch pants. Taller buyers report the leg runs short. Sizing opinions split: Nordstrom reviewers mostly call it true to size, and Vuori's own fit slider lands near the middle. Some REI buyers, however, advise sizing up, and one exchanged a medium for a large. Anyone who wants a looser, lounge-friendly fit should go up a size.

**Durability.** Opinion is mixed. Nordstrom reviewers say the fabric resists pilling and holds its shape, and many owners buy several pairs. Critical REI reviewers describe loose threads and, in one case, a logo that began peeling on day one, and complained that build quality doesn't match the price. A Kads Review write-up flags pilling with heavy wear, and one Nordstrom reviewer saw the black colorway fade at the thighs. Abrasion from rocks, brush and a pack hip belt are what a garment like this will not enjoy.

## Pros & Cons

**Pros**

- Soft, 4-way stretch knit that moves freely and holds its shape
- Slim, tapered cut with cuffed ankles that looks decent in town
- Front leg zip pockets and a zippered back pocket
- Quick-drying, moisture-wicking fabric
- Machine washable and easy to pack as a camp-and-travel layer

**Cons**

- About 280 g, heavy for a pair of pants that serves one purpose on trail
- $110 list price (frequently discounted during sales)
- Sizing is inconsistent: some owners size up
- 28-inch inseam runs short for taller wearers
- Mixed durability reports, including loose threads, pilling and fading
- Midweight knit is neither a true winter layer nor a rugged trail pant

## Who Should Buy This

The Sunday suits hikers and travelers who want one comfortable pair of pants for the airport, the trailhead town, camp evenings and the odd short day hike in mild weather. It also fits anyone who wants a gym-capable jogger that doesn't read as gym wear. Thru-hikers and gram-counters should look elsewhere: a lightweight nylon hiking pant or shorts plus a dedicated sleep layer will weigh less, resist abrasion better and shrug off weather more easily. Buyers on the tall side or those concerned about build quality should try a pair on first, or buy from a retailer with easy returns.

## Verdict

The Sunday Performance Jogger is one of the more comfortable and better-liked joggers available, and it earns that reputation as camp, travel and everyday wear. It is not a trail pant, it carries a weight penalty for backpacking, and quality-control complaints temper the otherwise glowing reviews. Buy it as a comfort piece at a sale price, not as core hiking kit.
