## Overview

The Outdoor Research Swift Cap is a 71g (2.5 oz) performance ball cap aimed at trail runners, thru-hikers, and anyone who spends long days in the sun. <cite index="1-2">It features a bluesign® approved recycled nylon blend that's highly breathable, water resistant, quick drying, and delivers UPF 40 sun protection for trail running and warm-weather hiking.</cite> It's a design OR has been refining for years — and the loyalty it commands (reviewers who own 10, 11, even 20+ of them) suggests they've landed on something that genuinely works.

## Performance

### Breathability & Temperature Management

The Swift Cap uses a layered construction that's smarter than it looks. <cite index="10-6">Three mesh panels completely cover the top of the head, providing breathability for coolness and wicking away dampness for comfort, while another three nylon panels rest above the forehead and scalp, ready to block wind and rain.</cite> That outer shell does add some warmth relative to a fully-open mesh cap, so if you run extremely hot, you may notice the cap retaining a little more heat than you'd like. <cite index="14-12">It's not as breathable as one might hope on a very hot day</cite> — that's an honest trade-off for a cap that also sheds light rain. If you're racing summer ultras and need maximum ventilation above all else, OR's Swift Air Cap pushes the mesh-to-nylon ratio further.

### Sweat Management

This is where the Swift Cap earns its following. <cite index="5-6">The quick-release back buckle allows for on-the-fly adjustment, and the moisture-wicking TransAction headband keeps perspiration off the face and eyes.</cite> <cite index="24-39">The design channels sweat away from the top, and the brim acts almost like a gutter, preventing sweat from dripping down onto your face.</cite> For high-output efforts on warm days, that's a meaningful quality-of-life feature.

### Sun Protection & Brim

UPF 40 is solid — not the maximum available, but adequate for most outdoor use. <cite index="5-5">The dark fabric under the brim helps reduce glare</cite>, a subtle but genuinely useful touch that cuts eye strain on snowfields, water, or dry, reflective trails. The brim is stiff and curved. <cite index="9-14">It has enough integrity to hold its shape, and the entire cap offers bounce-back malleability so you can crush and stuff it if the feeling strikes.</cite> After being crammed into a pack pocket for hours, it comes back to shape reliably.

### Fit & Adjustability

The Quick Release Buckle at the back dials in fit quickly. <cite index="11-7">Unlike some hats, the quick-release belt buckle is easy to adjust while on the move.</cite> That said, there's a recurring complaint worth flagging: <cite index="13-39,13-40">some long-time Swift Cap fans report that recent versions run significantly larger than older ones, with the adjustment buckle barely cinching down far enough for smaller heads.</cite> Additionally, <cite index="13-25,13-26">on very large heads (7½+ hat size), there's just enough excess strap to tuck, but it can come loose and flap around annoyingly.</cite> If you're ordering for the first time, buying from a retailer with a generous return policy is the pragmatic move.

### Durability & Packability

The Swift Cap holds up. <cite index="15-9,15-10,15-11,15-12">It takes a beating — including being crammed into mesh pack pockets on long multi-day hikes — and always comes out fine, standing up well to numerous washings.</cite> <cite index="2-3">It survives several machine washes without deforming.</cite> Care instructions call for cold wash and drip dry, which is easy enough to follow on trail via a stream rinse.

**Cons**

- Excess adjustment strap can dangle on larger heads with no clean keeper for the tail
- DWR is water resistant, not waterproof — fine for light drizzle, not sustained rain

## Who Should Buy This

The Swift Cap is a natural fit for the thru-hiker or warm-weather backpacker who wants one hat that handles everything: sun protection, sweat management, light rain, and helmet compatibility — without adding noticeable weight to the pack. <cite index="16-1">Designed for runners moving light and fast, it's actually brilliant for any activity where insulation is not required.</cite> It's equally at home on a long trail day or a marathon. If maximum breathability in summer heat is your top priority, look at the OR Swift Air Cap or similar mesh-heavy alternatives — but for a well-rounded three-season performer, the Swift Cap is hard to beat.

## Verdict

The Outdoor Research Swift Cap is one of the most field-tested, user-validated performance caps in the trail running and hiking space, and it earns that reputation honestly. The layered mesh-and-nylon construction, TransAction headband, and glare-cutting dark brim are thoughtful features that pay off on long days out. The sizing inconsistency and strap management are real — but minor — annoyances. At 71g and with a proven track record for durability, this cap belongs on any warm-weather packing list.

**Rating: 8.5 / 10**
